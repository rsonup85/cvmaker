Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a CV and a resume?

The terms are used differently in different countries and industries. The important part is to present relevant qualifications and experience clearly for the intended application.

Can I update my CV after creating it?

Yes. Keep your details current and review the document before each important application or submission.
